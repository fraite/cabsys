Thermal Resistivity of Soil (K.m/W)

Symbols: ρT, ρw, ρe

The soil thermal resistivity of the ground is defined in IEC 60287-2-2 Section 4.2.2 and Section 4.2.3. This assumes a uniform soil thermal resistivity in the ground.

IEC 60287-2-2 Section 4.2.7.4 does cater for cable surrounds, such as concrete duct blocks, with a different thermal resistivity to the soil (or earth). Methods such as conformal mapping (CIGRE ELECTRA 98) and finite element analysis also allow for multiple thermal resistivities. In these situations this value is the bulk soil thermal resistivity.

For a single isolated cable or circuit where partial drying-out of the soil occurs, see IEC 60287-2-1 Section 1.4.2, this value is the thermal resistivity of the moist soil.

The thermal resistivity of the soil is used to calculate the ground thermal resistance, T4, for current rating calculations.
