Thermal Resistivity of Dry Soil (K.m/W)

Symbols: ρd

The soil thermal resistivity of the dry ground is defined in IEC 60287-1-1 Section 1.4.2. This assumes a dry soil resistivity inside a critical temperature. Outside the critical temperature a wet soil thermal resistivity is used, in CabSys this being the same parameter as the (general) soil thermal resistivity

The calculation is only valid for a single circuit / cable set.

The thermal resistivity of the soil is used to calculate the ground thermal resistance, T4, for current rating calculations.
